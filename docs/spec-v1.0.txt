Executive summary
The product is an ESP32-based environmental controller for a meat drying or curing chamber. It measures and regulates temperature and relative humidity, controls internal circulation and fresh-air renewal, tracks multiple products by weight, records the drying history and raises clear alarms when the chamber cannot maintain safe operating conditions.
Two economy editions are specified. Economy Panel is the lowest-cost standalone controller with a small display and buttons. Economy Web adds an embedded browser interface, remote alarms, data export and firmware updates while keeping the complete electronics budget at or below EUR 200. Both editions share the same local safety-first control engine. The recommended Economy Web build uses carefully selected sensors, protected switching, a watchdog and safe recovery to improve reliability without creating a separate premium product line.

1. Scope and design principles
1.1 In scope
Closed-loop control of cooling, heating, humidification and dehumidification.
Scheduled or demand-based internal ventilation and fresh-air exchange.
Manual or automatic weight tracking for several products or batches.
Local physical interface on both editions; responsive web interface on Economy Web.
Remote monitoring and alarms without making cloud access necessary for control.
Data logging, configuration backup, diagnostics and firmware updates.
Two hardware profiles that run the same core control logic.
1.2 Out of scope
A guarantee of microbiological or food safety.
Automatic measurement of pH, salt concentration, nitrite concentration or water activity unless added later as supported external instruments.
Commercial HACCP certification or regulatory validation.
Direct control of mains loads without correctly rated relays, contactors, fuses, earthing and enclosure.
1.3 Design principles
DP-01 [MUST] Local control shall continue without Wi-Fi, Internet, MQTT, Home Assistant or any remote service.
DP-02 [MUST] Every actuator shall fail to a defined safe state after sensor failure, firmware restart or communication loss.
DP-03 [MUST] Cooling compressor protection shall be implemented locally, including minimum OFF time, minimum ON time and startup lockout.
DP-04 [MUST] Configuration and historical data shall survive power interruption.
DP-05 [MUST] The software shall expose the reason for each actuator decision, not only its ON/OFF state.
DP-06 [MUST] One application codebase shall support Economy Panel and Economy Web through build profiles and runtime hardware capability detection.

3. Functional requirements
3.1 Temperature control
FR-T-01 [MUST] Measure chamber air temperature at least every 5 seconds and display a filtered value and the latest raw value.
FR-T-02 [MUST] Support cooling, heating, or both, with independent configurable setpoint and deadband.
FR-T-03 [MUST] Prevent cooling and heating from operating simultaneously.
FR-T-04 [MUST] Provide compressor minimum OFF time (default 7 minutes), minimum ON time (default 3 minutes) and power-up lockout (default 2 minutes).
FR-T-05 [MUST] Support absolute high and low temperature limits. A limit breach shall stop any actuator that makes the condition worse.
FR-T-06 [SHOULD] Economy Web should compare the main temperature reading with an optional low-cost backup sensor and alarm on divergence.
3.2 Humidity control
FR-H-01 [MUST] Measure relative humidity at least every 10 seconds and calculate dew point and absolute humidity.
FR-H-02 [MUST] Support humidifier and dehumidifier outputs with independent deadband and minimum cycle times.
FR-H-03 [MUST] Prevent humidifier and dehumidifier from operating simultaneously.
FR-H-04 [MUST] Account for interaction with cooling and heating: cooling may reduce humidity and heating may reduce relative humidity.
FR-H-05 [MUST] Detect implausible, saturated, frozen or unavailable humidity readings and enter the configured safe state.
FR-H-06 [MUST] Allow field calibration against a reference instrument and retain calibration coefficients.
3.3 Internal ventilation
FR-V-01 [MUST] Control an internal circulation fan separately from fresh-air exchange.
FR-V-02 [MUST] Support continuous low-speed operation, interval operation and demand operation.
FR-V-03 [MUST] Allow a minimum and maximum duty cycle per programme phase.
FR-V-04 [MUST] Provide a door-open pause and a configurable post-door circulation cycle.
FR-V-05 [SHOULD] If speed feedback is available, alarm when the fan is commanded ON but no rotation is detected.
3.4 Fresh-air exchange
FR-A-01 [MUST] Control a dedicated intake/exhaust fan or motorised damper independently from the circulation fan.
FR-A-02 [MUST] Support fixed intervals, daily schedules, manual purge and optional CO2-based demand.
FR-A-03 [MUST] Delay or inhibit fresh-air exchange when it would worsen an active critical temperature or humidity deviation, unless a safety purge is required.
FR-A-04 [MUST] Track cumulative fresh-air runtime and the last exchange time.
3.5 Product and weight management
FR-W-01 [MUST] Support at least 20 active products and 100 archived products.
FR-W-02 [MUST] Store product name, type, batch, chamber position, start date/time, initial weight, current weight, target loss percentage and optional notes/photo reference.
FR-W-03 [MUST] Accept manual weigh-ins from the local or remote interface.
FR-W-04 [MUST] Support optional automatic weighing channels and associate each channel with one product or tray.
FR-W-05 [MUST] Calculate weight loss as: 100 x (initial weight - current weight) / initial weight.
FR-W-06 [MUST] Display initial weight, latest weight, absolute loss, percentage loss, drying rate per day and estimated completion date.
FR-W-07 [MUST] Retain the full weigh-in history. A new reading shall not overwrite earlier readings.
FR-W-08 [MUST] Detect negative loss, sudden implausible changes, disconnected load cells and long periods without a weigh-in.
FR-W-09 [MUST] Allow several products to share one programme while retaining separate progress and target values.

4.3 Control behaviour
Use hysteresis for the initial release. PID control may be added later only for proportional devices; it is not required for compressor relays.
Apply rolling median/outlier rejection followed by a light low-pass filter. Alarm logic must also examine raw readings to avoid hiding a rapid excursion.
Each output command records timestamp, requested state, actual state if feedback exists, decision reason and any blocking timer.
After reboot, outputs start OFF. Sensors must be valid for a configurable stabilisation period before automatic control resumes.
Manual commands expire automatically and remain subject to hard limits and compressor protection.

AL-03 [MUST] Actuator commanded ON without expected environmental response within a configurable time.

6. User interface and remote access
6.1 Local interface
UI-01 [MUST] Economy Web shall provide a responsive interface served by the ESP32 and usable from a phone without an application install.
UI-02 [MUST] Provide an initial Wi-Fi access-point setup flow and a physical method to re-enter setup mode.

UI-04 [MUST] Provide charts for temperature, humidity, dew point, output activity and weight loss.

UI-06 [MUST] Both editions shall include a small status display, three or four buttons, a status LED and a local alarm buzzer. Economy Web shall remain fully usable when Wi-Fi is unavailable.

8.3 Recommended reliability configuration
Recommendation
Use this configuration for Economy Web. It is not a separate product version: it is a better component selection and safer implementation that remains below the EUR 200 target.
 | 
Use an SHT45 as the main temperature/RH probe and a waterproof DS18B20 as an independent temperature cross-check.
Use a genuine ESP32-S3 module with hardware watchdog, brownout detection, protected boot settings and dual firmware partitions.
Keep regulation, alarms and the local panel independent from Wi-Fi, MQTT, Home Assistant and Internet availability.
Use opto-isolated relay outputs only as control signals; switch compressors and mains loads through correctly rated contactors with compressor minimum-off timing.
Use a certified power supply, input fuse, transient protection, replaceable sensor connectors and strain relief.
Write configuration atomically with CRC; use a microSD append journal for history and recover cleanly after power loss.
Fit an independent high/low safety thermostat where a temperature excursion could damage products or equipment.

13. Reliability and safety
13.1 Failure responses
Failure
 | Required response for both editions
 | 
Primary T/RH probe fails
 | Stop humidity control; use backup temperature only if validated; raise a local alarm and remote alarm on Economy Web
 | 
ESP32 restarts
 | Set outputs OFF, validate sensors, honour compressor lockout, record reset cause and resume only if configured

 |

13.2 Electrical and food-safety constraints
Mandatory implementation constraint
Mains wiring, fusing, earthing, contactor sizing and enclosure construction must be performed or reviewed by a competent person. Cheap PCB relays must not directly switch a compressor or other inductive mains load unless their real load rating and protection are verified.
 | 
Provide a physical emergency OFF or readily accessible isolator.
Use independent over-temperature/under-temperature protection for unattended operation.
Position sensors away from direct humidifier mist, cooling coil discharge and fan jet; protect them from condensation while retaining airflow.
Environmental control does not measure water activity or pH and cannot determine whether meat is safe to eat.
Recipes and alarm limits must be set by a competent user and must not be presented as validated food-safety advice.

 | Acceptance criterion
 |

 | Disconnect all networks for 24 h; regulation and logging continue and queued events recover.
 |

 | Remove each probe; fault detected within configured timeout; safe output state and alarm produced.

18. Open decisions before detailed design
Exact chamber type, internal volume and environmental range.
Cooling, heating, humidifying and dehumidifying equipment ratings and whether they restart automatically after power is restored.
Number of independently weighed products/trays and required weight resolution.
Need for fermentation temperatures above normal drying temperature.
Wi-Fi signal quality at the chamber and preferred secure remote-access method.
Required retention period and whether data must be synchronised to another system.
Whether the first release requires a local screen or may use a phone/web browser only.

Appendix B - Definition of done for the first build
Economy Panel and Economy Web reference units assembled in protected enclosures with correctly rated external switching.
Temperature, humidity, circulation and fresh-air outputs tested with simulated and real loads.
At least three products can be registered and weighed independently from the local panel; the same operations work through Economy Web.
Power failure, probe failure and network failure produce defined safe behaviour.
Thirty days of environmental and weight history can be viewed or exported.
A critical alarm is visible locally and reaches the configured remote destination.
Configuration and firmware can be backed up, updated and rolled back.
